# 👋 Hi, I'm Aries (aka Ariiies)

I'm a **Software Engineer** based in Guadalajara, Mexico — driven by **Python**, with **PHP/Laravel** as my daily craft. 

Currently working at **Netcommerce**, I architect, modernize, and maintain production web applications and internal systems (such as call-center platforms, ERPs, and high-traffic portals) for major clients across the **automotive, tech chamber, luxury retail, and specialized commerce industries**. I bridge the gap between maintaining critical **legacy systems (PHP 7.x)** and building modern full-stack solutions with **PHP 8+, Laravel, Filament, Livewire, and React**. 

Outside of work, I channel my passion into **Python and Rust** — creating open-source tools, designing search engines from scratch, and diving deep into the mathematical foundations of Data Science and Machine Learning.
